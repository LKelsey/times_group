Research

Scope

The astrophysics of transients, in both gravitational waves and light, and the inference methods built for those problems.

Gravitational waves

LVK · ET · LISA · PTA

The astrophysics of gravitational-wave sources, not tests of GR.

  • Compact binary populations and their stellar-evolution origins
  • Pulsars and neutron stars
  • Next-generation detectors and what they will tell us about sources

EM transients

Any wavelength · any transient

The astrophysics of explosive and variable sources, not cosmology.

  • Supernovae, kilonovae, GRBs and afterglows, TDEs, FBOTs and X-ray transients
  • Host galaxies of transients
  • Survey science with ZTF, LSST, Einstein Probe and JWST follow-up

Inference that connects to these

Methods applied to the problems

Specific applications of methods to transient and GW problems. This isn't the place for a generic algorithm.

  • Bayesian model fitting and surrogate models
  • Simulation-based inference
  • GPU-accelerated and differentiable pipelines in JAX

Agentic AI for transient science

AI agents as research tools

Using AI agents to do transient and GW science.

  • Agent-driven analysis workflows
  • Claude Code and tool-using agents for research
  • Decision-making for follow-up at survey scale
