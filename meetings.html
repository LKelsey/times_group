Logistics

Meetings

One hour a week, open to anyone. Here's when, where and how.

Formats

We alternate between three formats.

News + talk

Recent news and updates, then a 30-minute talk from a group member or a visitor.

Journal club

A detailed discussion of a few specific papers.

Round table

Everyone gives a quick update (bring a plot!) and we discuss.

We also run occasional tutorials on modelling, inference, agentic AI and data/observations. See Software.

Get involved

  1. Join the Slack channel. It's on the IoA Slack, so check you can access that workspace first.
  2. Have a visitor? If they want to speak and fit the scope, let us know and we'll find a slot.
  3. Want to present? Internal talks, progress updates and tutorials are all welcome.
  4. Seen a good paper? Post it on Slack for news or suggest it for journal club.
